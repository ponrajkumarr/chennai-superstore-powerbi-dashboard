# chennai-superstore-powerbi-dashboard
Retail sales and business performance dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI, featuring four analytical pages covering executive KPIs, store performance, sales and products, and customer and inventory analytics.
# Chennai Superstore – Power BI Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Chennai Superstore Power BI Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze retail sales, profitability, store performance, customer behavior, and inventory health.

The dashboard transforms retail data into actionable insights through KPI cards, interactive charts, and comparative analysis, helping business stakeholders monitor performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales, profit, and business growth.
- Compare the performance of five retail stores.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns and spending behavior.
- Monitor inventory value and stock-out rates.
- Support data-driven business decisions through interactive reporting.

## Dashboard Structure

### 01. Executive Overview

**Purpose:** Understand overall sales, profitability, and business health.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- Total Sales
- Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %
- Customers
- Sales Growth %

**Visualizations**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Store
- Sales by Category
- Top 10 Products

### 02. Store Performance

**Purpose:** Compare performance across all five stores and identify areas requiring attention.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- Store Sales
- Store Profit
- Store Growth %
- Target Achievement %
- Conversion %

**Visualizations**
- Sales Comparison Across Five Stores
- Profit Comparison Across Five Stores
- Monthly Sales Trend by Store
- Footfall vs. Transactions

### 03. Sales & Product Analysis

**Purpose:** Understand product performance, sales trends, and profitability drivers.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- Transactions
- Average Transaction Value
- Units Sold
- Product Profit
- Discount %

**Visualizations**
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Top 10 Products
- Bottom 10 Products

### 04. Customer & Inventory Analytics

**Purpose:** Analyze customer value, purchasing behavior, and inventory health.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
- Total Customers
- Average Customer Spend
- Purchase Frequency
- Stock Value
- Stock-out %

**Visualizations**
- New vs. Returning Customers
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Stock Value by Store
- Stock vs. Sales by Product

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations, business metrics, and time-based analysis
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships and analytical data structure
- **Retail Sales Data** – Sales, products, stores, customers, and inventory analysis

## Key Analytical Capabilities

- KPI tracking and performance monitoring
- Month-over-month and year-over-year sales analysis
- Store-level sales and profitability comparisons
- Product ranking and performance analysis
- Customer segmentation and retention analysis
- Inventory valuation and stock-out monitoring
- Interactive filtering and cross-visual analysis

## Business Value

This dashboard helps retail stakeholders:

- Identify high-performing and low-performing stores.
- Understand which products and categories contribute most to sales and profit.
- Track growth against previous periods and business targets.
- Evaluate customer spending and returning-customer behavior.
- Identify potential inventory risks and product-level sales imbalances.
- Make informed decisions about sales strategy, product performance, and inventory management.

## How to Use the Dashboard

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. If prompted, update the data source path to the location of the dataset.
4. Refresh the data if required.
5. Explore the four dashboard pages and interact with the available filters and visualizations.
